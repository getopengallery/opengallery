# Administration

Running the gallery as an administrator: site-wide settings, people and permissions, languages, the look of the site, plugins, the activity log and statistics.

- [Site settings](settings.md): name, logo, language, search engines and link previews, registration, what visitors see, uploads, e-mail and the activity log.
- [Users, roles and groups](users-and-permissions.md): accounts, what each role may do, and groups for sharing.
- [Languages](languages.md): the site's default language, each person's own language, and what is translated.
- [Themes](themes.md): choosing a theme and customizing colours, type, header, footer, albums, landing and login pages.
- [Plugins and sign-in](plugins.md): turning plugins on and off, and single sign-on with identity providers.
- [The dashboard](dashboard.md): the panels of the admin's first page and who sees which.
- [Dashboard and activity log](activity.md): figures and the record of logins, views, downloads and changes.
- [Statistics](statistics.md): visits, downloads, disk space, growth, member activity and who can see what.
