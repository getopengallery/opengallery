# User guide

This guide covers everything you do through the running gallery: installing and upgrading, the admin settings, albums and photos, sharing, profiles, themes, pages, permissions and the activity log. It is written for site owners, administrators and the people who look after albums.

New here? Start with [Installing and upgrading](getting-started/installation.md) and then [First steps](getting-started/first-steps.md).

## Contents

- [Getting started](getting-started/README.md): installing and upgrading, your first steps, and finding your way around the admin.
- [Albums, photos and pages](content/README.md): albums, the media library, sharing and content pages.
- [Administration](administration/README.md): site settings, users and roles, languages, themes, plugins and the activity log.
- [Members and visitors](members-and-visitors/README.md): the site as visitors see it, logging in, "My albums" and your profile.

## Words used in this guide

- **Admin** or **admin area**: the management area at `/admin`, for people with the permission "Open the admin area".
- **Site**: what visitors and members see.
- **Owner**: the person an album belongs to.
- **Staff**: people with the permission "See every album", by default administrators and moderators.
